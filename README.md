Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb) ($2 prize pool, top 10 paid).

`main.py` writes one sentence (134 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> A spider's web spun round the earth, where one tug on a thread carries your words to any house and brings the answer back in a breath.

The sentence explains the modern internet to a traveler from 1800 using
only things they could picture — a spider's web, a tug on a thread, words
carried between houses, an answer returning in a breath — with no word
coined after their time and no list of features.
